Relative time

Keep an absolute date in semantic time markup. The optional script enhances it with a localized relative label.

Example timestamp

October 10, 2026 at 3:30 PM EDT

Invalid date fallback

10 October 2026, 19:30 UTC

Markup contract

<script type="module" src="/dist/relative-time.js"></script>

<lw-relative-time lang="en" time-zone="America/New_York">
  <time datetime="2026-10-10T15:30:00-04:00">
    <span data-relative-time-fallback>October 10, 2026 at 3:30 PM EDT</span>
    <span data-relative-time-output hidden></span>
  </time>
</lw-relative-time>

The server-rendered absolute label remains visible without JavaScript, for dates more than 30 calendar days away, and for invalid dates or unsupported locale/time-zone options. After enhancement it stays available to assistive technology while the relative label is shown, and the exact date is available by mouse hover. Date-only values keep their calendar meaning. Keep the original offset in datetime for timestamps. The optional time-zone attribute controls calendar-day boundaries; without it, the viewer's local time zone is used. Relative labels refresh on one shared second-aligned timer that stops when all instances are removed.
