People picker

Choose who belongs to something. Group toggles represent whole circles, each row has its own checkbox, and an optional trailing column holds one control per person.

These are static presentation examples. Native checkboxes, switches and role fields work locally; group changes, Clear selection, More people and saving need application handlers. See the people picker manual for the submission contract.
