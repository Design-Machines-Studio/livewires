Relative time

Show how recent something is while keeping its absolute date available.

Example

Last updated October 10, 2026 at 3:30 PM EDT.

Without JavaScript, for dates over 30 days from today, or when the date, locale, or time-zone option is invalid, the absolute label stays visible. The original datetime value retains its offset and precise instant.

Use it

Load the optional /dist/relative-time.js entry only where this element is used. Put the localized absolute date in data-relative-time-fallback, and leave the output span empty. Set lang and optional time-zone on each wrapper when the page defaults do not express the desired locale or calendar-day boundary.

<lw-relative-time lang="en" time-zone="America/New_York">
  <time datetime="2026-10-10T15:30:00-04:00">
    <span data-relative-time-fallback>October 10, 2026 at 3:30 PM EDT</span>
    <span data-relative-time-output hidden></span>
  </time>
</lw-relative-time>

The module uses Intl.RelativeTimeFormat, keeps the exact absolute date available to assistive technology and by mouse hover, recalculates relative labels on one shared second-aligned timer, and clears that timer after the final instance disconnects. Dates more than 30 calendar days away use the absolute label instead.
